Audited themes

Loud but shallow

Price fairness

Raised by 3 of 6 · no first-hand overcharge

It sounded like a top theme. P06's line that unconnected older residents "get charged four hundred pounds for a job worth eighty" is the most memorable in the interviews. On audit, only 3 participants raised it, and no one described an overcharge that actually happened to them. P06 heard it secondhand ("I hear about it afterwards from a daughter or a son"), and P03's daughter describes a fear, not an event: "She'd pay it and not tell me."

Problem statements, ranked

Each statement is built from four parts. Pick a part to isolate it across all three statements.

Highlight
1

Requesters struggle to find trusted providers outside their existing network

WhoRequesters with few local connections Whatoften have no one to ask when they need someone to work in their home. WhyBecause trust in local providers is largely built through personal recommendations, they may have to rely on sources they trust less or wait until someone happens to recommend a provider. ImpactThis is a problem because requesters may delay getting help or avoid booking altogether, limiting Vello's ability to serve people who do not already have a strong local network.

2

Providers struggle to reach customers beyond their existing network

WhoProviders who rely on word of mouth Whathave limited ways to reach customers outside their existing referral network. WhyTheir ability to grow depends on how many people already know and recommend them, rather than simply on the quality of their work. ImpactThis is a problem because good providers can struggle to find new customers, limiting the supply available on Vello and making it harder for the marketplace to grow.

3

Requesters are less likely to trust a provider based on platform checks alone

WhoRequesters Whatoften place more trust in a recommendation from someone they know than in checks or assurances provided by a company. WhyWhen there is no personal recommendation behind a provider, requesters may continue looking for help through their existing networks instead of booking through Vello. ImpactThis is a problem because Vello may struggle to turn new requesters into bookings and repeat users, limiting the value of the app.
