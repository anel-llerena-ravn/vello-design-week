How product works with design

Two tools from the week. The handoff checklist is a gate: nothing moves to design until every box is checked. The design-support checklist is how product works alongside design in every phase, so design can do its best work.

Brief to design

A gate for the moment work changes hands between product and design. Every box is checked before the work moves on. If a box can't be checked, it's either fixed or written down as an open question with an owner and a default answer.

Product design-support checklist

Design owns the solution and the final UX call. Product's job is to make sure design is solving the right problem, with the right evidence, inside clear limits, and to catch weak decisions before they reach the user.

Guesses are messes.

Every gap product leaves in a conversation, design fills with a guess, so communication is direct, written down and early.
