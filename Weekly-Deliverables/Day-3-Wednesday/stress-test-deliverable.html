Where I corrected Claude

Claude's first draft

Assumptions like "Posting can't fail" and "Loads instantly, never fails".

Correction

We can't assume that. The flow has to plan for specific scenarios, like the internet connection dropping mid request creation.

Gaps, from most to least critical

Missing state
1

What if Sam's address is inside the neighborhood, but no verified dog walker covers that street?

What the flow did
Sam gets "Request posted to your block" and "Neighbors usually answer within a couple of hours" anyway.
Missing state
Empty: no walkers nearby
What Sam should see
S5 says no walkers cover the street yet, keeps the request open for new walkers, and offers Browse neighbors.
2

What if Sam loses connection right as they tap Post?

What the flow did
One note says posting waits for a connection and another says it fails, so Sam can't tell if Loki's request went out.
Missing state
Error: not sure it posted
What Sam should see
A clear message saying whether the request is live or still waiting to post, and trying again never posts it twice.
3

What if Elena replies and is then removed by a Community Admin before Sam opens the replies?

What the flow did
Only a walker who withdraws is covered, so Elena's reply would still show as verified and bookable.
Missing state
Error: reply no longer available
What Sam should see
Elena's reply shows "No longer available on Vello", Book is off, and the reply count drops.
4

What if Sam posts a second dog-walking request by mistake?

What the flow did
S2 lets Sam post another without saying what happens to the first, and the prototype replaces it along with its replies.
Missing state
Conflict: two open requests
What Sam should see
S2 says the first request and its replies stay, and Sam can see both requests and delete the extra one.
5

What if no walker ever replies?

What the flow did
S5 keeps saying "usually within a couple of hours" for days, and the 24-hour nudge has no screen.
Missing state
Empty: still no replies after a day
What Sam should see
After 24 hours S5 explains that nobody has replied yet and offers to widen the time or budget, or browse neighbors.

Every screen in the flow lists its Loading, Empty, Error and Success states. These are design requirements, so they aren't counted as assumptions.

Findings

One gap between the flow and Vello's current screens

Assumption #6: who gets the request

The prototype always shows "Request posted to your block", whether or not a walker covers Sam's street.

The flow adds a check (D6), and if no walker covers the street, S5 tells Sam and keeps the request open.

One place the journey reveals a need a screen alone wouldn't

The Wait stage

Looking at S5 alone, the problem looks like an empty state. The journey shows that Sam isn't looking at S5: Sam is at work, hopeful but uncertain.

"Will anyone be free every single weekday?"
  • The problem: between posting and the first reply, Sam has no way to know whether anything is happening, and Vello says nothing unless Sam opens the app.
  • Why a screen misses it: the problem only appears over time, and a single screen doesn't show time passing.
  • Ideas to explore: telling Sam when a walker replies, checking in when a day goes by with no replies, or showing how many walkers have seen the request.
