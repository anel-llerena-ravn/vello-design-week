Part 1

Upcoming

The list the requester opens from the Bookings tab. Maya's weekday walks are confirmed. Devon's TV mount is still waiting on a reply.

3 from the log 3 taste slips

  1. 3FindingU1, U4Both

    The status is the hardest thing to read on the card

    Can the requester see which bookings are on without reading every card?

    Status is the smallest text on the card (11px). “Waiting on reply” is 2.84:1, below 4.5:1.

  2. 4FindingU3, B4Both

    The price changes between the card and the booking screen

    Does the requester know what they'll actually pay?

    “$24 / walk” here becomes “Total $132.00” on the detail screen (per week, fee included).

  3. 7FindingU2, U4Both

    Buttons are too small to tap reliably

    Can the requester tap the right button on the first try, even on the move?

    Card buttons are 36px tall, 9px apart. Vello's rule is 44px.

  4. T1Taste slipManual pass

    Empty space on the right of each card doesn't look standardized

    Why it's taste
    Layout preference. No evidence it slows anyone down.
    Action
    Dropped
  5. T2Taste slipManual pass

    Manage and Message look too similar

    Why it's taste
    Visual impression. The screen shows a filled button next to an outlined one.
    Action
    Re-anchored: both buttons lead to Message
  6. T4Taste slipManual pass

    Good hierarchy between name, task and date

    Why it's taste
    Praise based on a read of the screen, with no evidence.
    Action
    Left out of the log

Part 2

Your booking

What opens after tapping “Manage”. On the left, Devon's booking that hasn't been accepted. On the right, the cancel sheet on Maya's weekday series.

6 from the log

  1. 1FindingU1, B1Me

    Devon's “Waiting on reply” booking shows as “Confirmed”

    Can the requester trust that a confirmed booking is really happening?

    “Manage” opens every booking as confirmed: green “You're all set” banner and “Confirmed” badge, even when Devon hasn't answered.

  2. 2FindingB1Me

    “Message Devon” says thanks without doing anything

    When the app says something worked, can the requester believe it?

    Shows “Message sent” without opening the chat. “Message” on the list opens the real thread.

  3. 4FindingU3, B4Both

    The price changes between the card and the booking screen

    Does the requester know what they'll actually pay?

    Devon's card says $65. Here it's “Total $71.50”, with the 10% fee added and not shown.

  4. 5FindingU2, B4Claude

    There's no way to change one walk, and cancel doesn't say one or all

    Can the requester change a recurring booking without cancelling all of it?

    “Cancel Maya's visit?” on a 5-walks-a-week series. It doesn't say if it's Monday or the whole series, or if there's a fee.

  5. 7FindingU2, U4Both

    “Cancel booking” is too small to tap reliably

    Can a stressed requester find and tap cancel on the first try?

    About a 22px tap area. It turns red only on hover, and phones have no hover.

  6. 8What worksB4, U2Me

    The cancel sheet makes keeping the booking the easy choice

    Does the cancel sheet help Vello avoid last-minute cancellations without trapping the requester?

    “Keep booking” is the main button, with a warning about the 24-hour penalty.

Part 3

Past

Finished and cancelled bookings. Where the requester rates a neighbor or books them again.

3 from the log 2 taste slips

  1. 6FindingB2, B5Both

    The loudest “Book again” is on the cancelled card

    Does the Past tab lead the requester back to the neighbors they liked?

    Filled on Sofia's cancelled card, ghost on Priya's 5-star visit. Sofia is also marked unavailable.

  2. 2FindingB1, B3Both

    “Leave a rating” says thanks without doing anything

    Is Vello actually getting the ratings its trust model needs?

    No stars are collected. The button stays on the card and can be tapped again.

  3. 4FindingU3, B4Me

    The price changes between the card and the booking screen

    Does the requester know what they paid?

    Marcus's one-off dog walk shows “$22” with no unit. “/ walk” only appears on weekly bookings.

  4. T3Taste slipManual pass

    Book again looks like plain text

    Why it's taste
    Perception, not tested.
    Action
    Kept only the checkable part: Book again has a different style on each card (#6)
  5. T4Taste slipManual pass

    The date format on Past helps scanning

    Why it's taste
    Praise based on a read of the screen, with no evidence.
    Action
    Left out of the log
