Bookings: Upcoming, Past and Manage

Bookings breaks two rules of our own design system.

Vello's design system promises tap targets of at least 44pt and 4.5:1 contrast on all text. The main buttons on every booking card are 36pt, and some status text drops to 2.3:1. Both issues come from shared components, so one fix to each component fixes it everywhere it appears.

Critical1 of 3 · Touch targets

Every booking card action is too small to tap reliably

Manage, Message, Leave a rating and Book again are the only ways to act on a booking. All four use the small button size, 8pt short of the minimum, which leads to missed taps and wrong-button taps for people with limited dexterity or a phone in one hand.

Recommendation

Fix the shared components, not the screens

Every critical issue lives in a component used across the app: the small button, the faded card state and the status badge. Fixing them there brings Bookings in line with the design system and fixes the same problems on other screens.

  1. Use the medium button size on booking cards
  2. Replace the faded cancelled state with the existing badge
  3. Darken the amber and neutral badge text

How this was measured

Contrast
Hex values from the prototype's design tokens, ratios from the WCAG luminance formula. Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA, 1.4.3).
Touch targets
Heights from the component CSS. The prototype renders at 1x, so 1px equals 1pt. Target 44pt (Apple HIG, WCAG 2.5.5).
Color alone
Every status, state and indicator reviewed for a second cue besides color (WCAG 1.4.1).
Scope
Upcoming and Past lists, and the screen opened by "Manage". The cancel flow and new bookings were out of scope.

Measured from code, not on a device, so touch target heights are close estimates. Booking data is sample data from the prototype.
